## Medical Insurance Policy – The main contract showing:
- Policyholder/client details
- Covered members/dependants
- Benefits and limits
- Premiums
- Exclusions
- Policy period
- Terms and conditions

## Certificate of Insurance / Insurance Certificate
 – A shorter document confirming that the client has active medical insurance. It may show the policy   number, insured person, coverage period, and insurer.

 ## Medical Insurance Card 
 – Used by the insured member when visiting hospitals or other healthcare providers. It typically    contains the member/policy number and insurer details.
